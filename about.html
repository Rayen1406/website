Our Story

Tunisia's Wholesale Spare Parts Partner

Itkan Automotive is Tunisia's leading wholesale supplier of premium automotive spare parts, serving spare parts shops across the country. We are committed to providing genuine-quality replacement parts for over 16 major car manufacturers at competitive wholesale prices.

Our extensive portfolio covers everything from engine internals to electrical components, ensuring that Tunisian spare parts shops can stock exactly what their customers need — reliably and at the best prices.

With a focus on quality, wholesale pricing, and exceptional B2B service, Itkan Automotive has established itself as the go-to wholesale partner for spare parts shops across Tunisia.
